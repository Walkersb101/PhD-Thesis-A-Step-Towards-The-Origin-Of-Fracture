\subsection{Notation}
Denote vectors and tensors in bold, such as the position vector $\mathbf{r}$ or the stress tensor $\mathbf{\Sigma}$.

non bold symbols denote scalars such as the volume fraction $\phi$.

Given a bold symbol the corresponding non-bold symbol, in the absence of a sub- or superscript specifying a component, denotes its magnitude, i.e. $r := |\mathbf{r}|$.

Bold symbols with the subscript denote the relative version of the vector between nodes $i$ and $j$, for example $\mathbf{r}_{ij} := \mathbf{r}_j - \mathbf{r}_i$ denotes the relative position of node $j$ with respect to particle $i$.
