\chapter{Simulation Ensemble Sizes}
\label{app:FatigueSampleCounts}

The results in \cref{sec:Fatigue} are obtained from ensembles of independent simulations, each generated with a different random-number seed. For the principal parameter sweeps, more than $10{,}000$ realisations were used for each parameter combination, although the precise ensemble size varies slightly with $N$, $l_0$, $l_w$, and $\gamma_0$. The $N=2048$ data are an exception and contain $2{,}500$ realisations because of their greater computational cost.
